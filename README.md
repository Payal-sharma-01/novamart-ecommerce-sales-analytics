## 🎯 PROJECT OVERVIEW

**NOVAMART** is an end-to-end E-Commerce Data Analytics project designed to evaluate business performance through transactional sales data.

The project analyzes sales revenue, profitability, customer behavior, product categories, geographical performance, and monthly sales trends.

Using **WPS Spreadsheet / Excel, MySQL, and Python**, raw transactional data is transformed into structured information, analytical findings, and interactive business dashboards.

The primary objective is to understand business performance and identify opportunities for better decision-making.

---

## 🎯 BUSINESS OBJECTIVES

The analysis focuses on answering important business questions:

- How is overall sales and profit performance?
- Which product categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories contribute most to profitability?
- How do monthly sales trends change over time?
- Which cities contribute significantly to sales?
- How can customer and order data support business decisions?
- What opportunities exist to improve overall performance?
